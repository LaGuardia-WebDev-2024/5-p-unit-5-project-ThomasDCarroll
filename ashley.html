Prologue

Ashley is part of the prank, but doesnt really have a central role. Her and Matt are kind of just there...

The Beginning

Ashley gets to the lodge with the rest of her friends. The first time we play as her, we see Mike and Emily hugging through a telescope. She can chose to tell Matt (Emily's boyfriend), showing her inability to make simple decisions... (yes that was me shading her lmao). She then goes to the lodge, watches Em and Jess fight, and then plays with a ouija board before being knocked out after her and Chris walk downstairs.

The Middle

Once Ashley wakes up, she is tied to a wall, with a spinning blade in front of her. Chris ends up slicing Josh, and they both get knocked out again. This time, Ashley wakes up across from Chris, who has a gun in his hand. The two are stitting under more girating sawblades, and Chris can chose to save himself or Ashley before the time runs out. This will become important later. .

The End

Once Ashley wakes up (she cant die at this point), Josh reveals that hes the killer, and that everything was a sick game. She then becomes a bystander in the crew before the end of the game where she waits for Chris to come back after he runs outside to help Josh. If Chris chooses to save himself, Ashley kills him. Yeah. Anyways, after this Ashley can once again make a dumb decision, being able to follow Jess' voice into the mines and abandoning the rest of the group. She then becomes setdressing for the rest of the game.

Review



Ashleys... cute... but her inability to make smart decisions and the way she carries herself annoyed me alot throughout the game. I can just tell Ashley would be one of those sensitive mean girls. Not to mention she can kill someone. Like, hello???

Ashley's Score: 2/10
