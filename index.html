Release

Until Dawn was released on August 25, 2015, with a remake being released over 9 years later on October 4, 2024. The game is known for revolutionizing choice-based games through it's usage of the butterfly effect.

Gameplay

The game is a fixed-camera decision-based horror game, where the player(s) has to use their intuition to keep 8 teenagers alive after they get abandonded at a snowy lodge. The remake opted to remove some of the fixed camera angles in favor of more cinematic angles.
